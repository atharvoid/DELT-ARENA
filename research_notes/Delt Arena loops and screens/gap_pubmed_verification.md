## Corrections to the plan

1. training_science.md line 39: replace "2-3 min rest between sets" with 3-5 min for heavy strength sets and 1-2 min for hypertrophy (ACSM 2009). Line 47 says the 2-10% load rule could not be confirmed; the ACSM abstract confirms it.
2. training_science.md Takeaway (line 8) and the bullet at line 14: the frequency evidence conflicts (Schoenfeld 2016 favours twice weekly; Grgic 2019 finds no pronounced effect under volume equality). Do not present twice-weekly as settled.
3. Weekly set range: training_science.md line 21 uses 10-20, concept/delt-arena.html line 604 uses 12-20, and the report's band (line 323) uses 10 to 20. Pick one, label it as practitioner synthesis, and drop any attribution of a range to Schoenfeld 2017. Baz-Valle supports 12-20 only for young trained men, with the triceps exception.
4. Load papers: the notes for Schoenfeld 2017 (PubMed 28834797) should state that every included study trained to failure. The same restriction applies to Lopez 2021.
5. Report line 414: remove "with less fatigue", which no checked abstract supports. Describe the failure vs non-failure difference as trivial (ES 0.19), not as "nearly the same growth".
6. Report line 333: change "no further muscle gain" to "no further fat-free mass gain from protein supplementation above about 1.6 g/kg/day".
7. training_science.md line 91 (Iraki): replace the "secondary sites" and "could not be verified" wording with the primary abstract figures. Keep the narrative-review caveat.
8. Bickel 2011: remove the "over 60" threshold as a finding, or mark it as untested design judgement (report line 397). The re-entry percentages and "re-entry weeks after a gap" (report lines 263 and 493) have no source in any checked paper; label them as design judgement.
9. Bell 2023: the "one week most common" claim (training_science.md line 40; report line 400) is not in the abstract. Check the full text or drop it.
10. training_science.md line 88 (ISSN): remove the "secondary summary" caveat and fix the typo.
11. Schoenfeld et al. 2017 (PubMed 27433992): the "more than 10 sets gives no clear benefit" wording in training_science.md line 11 is not in the abstract. Check the full text before keeping it.
12. Report line 269: "self-reported RIR is usually within about one rep of the truth" rests on a Stronger by Science page, not a paper. Find a primary validation study (Emanuel 2022, PubMed 36273064, is a candidate) or soften the claim.
13. TRIMP and calorie/MET figures are not PubMed sources and none was verified here. Affected: Banister and Edwards constants (stats_analytics.md line 61), Compendium MET values (report lines 355-358), the Concept2 calorie constant (stats_analytics.md line 58, which the notes already call inconsistent), and the elliptical overestimate (report line 360). Mark them unverified or check them before shipping.
